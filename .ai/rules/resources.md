# Resources

## Do not use eyebrow labels
Do not place small uppercase kicker or eyebrow text above page or section headings in Orbit. Lead with the heading itself and use supporting text only when it adds useful information.

## Keep shared UI styles consistent
Keep new and edited Orbit UI consistent with the rest of the app. Reuse shared button variants and sizes, form controls, and dialog styling; avoid one-off overrides for their radii, spacing, typography, and states.
